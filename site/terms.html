What the app is, and isn't

Kharcha Paani helps you keep track of what you spend and of who owes whom among friends. It records money; it doesn't move it. Payments by UPI happen in your own payment app, between you and your friend. We're not a bank, a payment service, a lender or a financial adviser, and nothing in the app is financial advice.

Balances, splits and AI suggestions are only as right as what's entered. Amounts that Google Gemini reads from a bill, a statement or a typed line can be wrong: check them before you save. Settle up based on what you and your friends agree, not only on what the app shows.

What you enter is yours. You let us store and process it only to run the app for you and the people you share groups with, as described in the privacy policy.
