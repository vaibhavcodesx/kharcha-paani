Kharcha Paani privacy policy

Last updated 25 October 2026.

Kharcha Paani is an app for tracking what you spend and splitting it with friends. It's made and run by Vaibhav Chauhan ("we", "us"). This page says what the app collects, why, who else handles it, and what you can do about it. It's written to be read, not skimmed past. If anything here is unclear, write to vaibhav.alt.chauhan@gmail.com.

Kharcha Paani is for people who are 18 or older. Please don't use it if you're younger.

What the app collects, and why

Your account. You sign in with Google. We receive your name, your email address and the link to your Google profile picture, and keep them with your account so you can sign in again and so your friends in shared groups can see who you are. We never see your Google password.

Your profile. The name, emoji and colour you choose, and your UPI ID if you add one. People you share a group with can see these, so they know who paid and can pay you back by UPI.

What you log. Expenses and income (amount, category, merchant or title, payment method, date, note), splits, IOUs, settlements and the groups you create or join. These are stored on your phone and, so they're safe if you lose it and so your groups stay in step, in your account in the cloud.

Friends who aren't on the app. When you split with someone who doesn't use Kharcha Paani, the name you gave them and what you split with them are stored in your account, visible only to you.

What stays on your phone only. Bill photos you attach, your subscriptions list, your own categories, the daily reminder settings and your notification choices. A backup file you export contains these too; where you keep that file is up to you.

Things we don't collect. We don't read your SMS, contacts, location, microphone or files, and the app asks for no permission to do so. Photos and PDFs reach the app only when you pick or share them. Voice entry uses your phone's own speech service; the app receives only the words. There are no ads and no ad tracking.

Who else handles your data

We use a small number of services to run the app. Each one handles your data only to do its job for us.

  • Supabase stores your account and everything that syncs (your profile, entries, groups, settlements). Our Supabase project is hosted in Tokyo, Japan (AWS region ap-northeast-1). Data is sent over encrypted connections, and the database only lets each person read what they're allowed to: their own entries, and the groups they're in.
  • Google Gemini reads what you choose to have read: a quick-add line you type or speak, a bill photo, a bank or UPI statement, or the summary of your spending behind an Insights question or a trip read. That's how the app fills in amounts for you. While Kharcha Paani is free it uses Gemini's free tier, so Google may use what's sent to improve its products, and Google's reviewers may see it. The app tells you this once, before the first time anything is sent, and you can always type entries yourself instead: everything else works the same. What Gemini reads is never shown to other users.
  • Google Sign-In confirms who you are when you sign in.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google) receives a report when the app crashes or hits a serious error: what went wrong in the code, the app version and the phone model and Android version. It never includes your amounts, names, notes or documents. You can turn crash reports off in the app under Help & about.

Your friends. In a shared group, the other members see your profile (name, emoji, colour, UPI ID if you added one) and the group's expenses, splits and payments. When you send an "ask to pay" message, you send it yourself, through WhatsApp or whichever app you pick; we don't send anything to your friends on your behalf.

We never sell your data, and we don't share it with anyone for advertising.

How long we keep it

Your data stays in your account while you have one. When you delete your account, everything of yours is removed from our systems straight away, except what's needed to keep your groups' history correct for the other members (see below), and copies in Supabase's backups, which are overwritten within 30 days.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app (account sheet → Delete my account) or, if you no longer have the app, by following the steps at the account deletion page linked from the app's store listing.

When you delete your account:

  • Your personal entries, private splits, friends who aren't on the app, and your profile details (email, UPI ID, picture, emoji, colour) are deleted, and you can't sign in to that account again.
  • Shared expenses and payments stay in your groups, because your groupmates' balances depend on them. On those, you choose how you appear: under your name as it is now, or as "Former member". If you don't choose, it's "Former member". A kept name is only the name, nothing else about you.
  • Groups you created pass to the member who has been in them longest; a group with nobody left is deleted.
  • Data on your phone is deleted when the app is removed or when you delete the account from it.

Your choices and rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us what personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and withdraw your consent. In the app you can:

  • correct your profile at any time;
  • type entries yourself instead of having them read by Gemini;
  • turn off crash reports, and choose which notifications you get;
  • delete your account and its data.

For anything else, including a complaint (a grievance), write to our grievance contact:

Vaibhav Chauhan, vaibhav.alt.chauhan@gmail.com

We reply within 90 days, and usually much sooner. If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Security

Your data travels over encrypted connections and is stored with the access rules described above. No system is perfectly secure; if something happens that puts your data at risk, we'll tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app uses your data, we'll update this page and its date, and tell you in the app before the change takes effect when it matters to you.
