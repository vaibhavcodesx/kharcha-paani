## What the app is, and isn't

Kharcha Paani helps you keep track of what you spend and of who owes whom among friends. It **records**
money; it doesn't **move** it. Payments by UPI happen in your own payment app, between you and your
friend. We're not a bank, a payment service, a lender or a financial adviser, and nothing in the app
is financial advice.

Balances, splits and AI suggestions are only as right as what's entered. Amounts that Google Gemini
reads from a bill, a statement or a typed line can be wrong: check them before you save. Settle up
based on what you and your friends agree, not only on what the app shows.

## Using it fairly

Don't use the app to harass anyone, to pretend to be someone else, to record things that aren't true
in a shared group, or to try to get at other people's data or break the service. We may suspend an
account that does.

What you enter is yours. You let us store and process it only to run the app for you and the people
you share groups with, as described in the privacy policy.

always be available or free of mistakes, and it's provided "as is". To the extent the law allows, we

We may change or stop features. If we ever plan to stop the service, we'll tell you in the app in good
