# Delete your Kharcha Paani account

Last updated 25 October 2026.

## In the app (quickest)

Open Kharcha Paani → tap your picture at the top → **Delete my account**, and follow the two steps.
If some of your changes haven't synced yet, the app asks you to sync first or to discard them.

## Without the app

If you've uninstalled the app, email **vaibhav.alt.chauhan@gmail.com** from the Google account you
signed in with, with the subject **"Delete my account"**. Say whether your name may stay on past group
expenses, or whether you'd rather be shown as "Former member" (the default if you don't say). We
delete the account within **7 days** of your email and reply to confirm.

## What's deleted, and what stays

**Deleted:** your account (you can't sign in to it again), your profile details (email, UPI ID,
picture, emoji, colour), your personal expenses and income, your private splits, and friends who
aren't on the app. Copies in our database backups are overwritten within 30 days.

**Stays:** expenses and payments in shared groups, because your groupmates' balances depend on them.
They show your name as it is now, or "Former member", as you choose. Only the name stays, nothing
else about you.

Groups you created pass to the member who has been in them longest; a group with nobody left is
deleted.
