# Play review: how the reviewer gets in (A-08, P-02)

Google's review team cannot sign in with a Google account they do not control. This page is what goes into
Play Console > App content > App access, and the steps the owner does once. An agent never creates the account.

## What the reviewer can do without any account

The app opens in local mode ("Use without signing in"): everything personal works with no account: add
expenses by typing, track subscriptions and local friends, split with local friends, Insights from local data, and
export. Sign-in is optional. Say this first in the App access answer: "All core features work without
signing in. A test account is provided for the features that need one (groups shared with other people, backup,
AI reads)."

## The test account (owner creates it, one time, about 15 minutes)

1. Create a new Google account used only for review (not your own). Turn on 2-step verification and keep the
   recovery codes in your password manager. Do not use a real person's name.
2. On a phone, sign in to the app with it. Set the display name to "Play Reviewer".
3. Seed it (about 10 minutes, so there is something to look at):
   - a group "Goa trip" with the account plus two local friends, with three expenses split three ways;
   - one pending settlement to confirm;
   - a bill photo split by items in progress (take a photo of any printed bill; do not use a real person's bill);
   - two personal expenses, one subscription.
4. In Play Console > App content > App access, choose "All or some functionality is restricted", add one
   instruction set: the account's email and password (a long random one), and the text below.
5. Keep the account's data tidy: do not delete it before the review ends; check that it still opens a week later.

## Text for the App access box (paste, then fill the two values)

> Sign in: tap "Continue with Google" and use the test account below. The password is not needed on the phone if
> Google asks you to choose an account; use these credentials only when Google asks to add an account.
> Email: <email>  Password: <password>
> No sign-in is needed for the core: tap "Use without signing in".
> AI reads: tap "Bill photo" on Home to read a bill, or the microphone bar for voice entry (the app explains the
> microphone before asking). AI can be turned off in Settings, and the app keeps working.
> Delete account: Settings > Delete my account. The web path is https://vaibhavcodesx.github.io/kharcha-paani-site/delete-account.html
> Money: the app does not hold or move money. Paying opens the person's own UPI app.

## Rotating the password

Change the review account's password after Google confirms the review result (a pass, or a rejection with reasons),
and put the new one in your password manager.
